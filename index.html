Fig. 2 — Me and My Future

Biography

Academic Background

I am a senior-year Physics student at Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra, pursuing an Integrated M.Sc. in Physics with a minor in Mathematics and Computing. My interests lie primarily in astronomy and cosmology — particularly galaxies, dark matter, astroparticle physics, dark energy, modified gravity, gravitational waves, stellar evolution, and the large-scale structure of the universe. I enjoy exploring these fields through both observational and computational approaches.

Current Direction

At present, I am actively seeking internships and research opportunities in these domains. I am also considering applying for one-year postgraduate diploma or research-oriented programs in theoretical physics or cosmology rather than continuing along a conventional academic route. Institutes such as Perimeter Institute and ICTP especially inspire me because of their emphasis on foundational physics, open scientific thought, and deep curiosity about the universe.

Long-Term Vision

In the long term, I hope to pursue a Ph.D. in computational cosmology, ideally combining astrophysics, cosmology, numerical simulations, and theoretical physics. More than anything, I am driven by curiosity — trying to understand how humanity interprets the universe while quietly adding a few ideas of my own along the way.

Supervisor: Dr. Suman Ghosh, Department of Physics, BIT Mesra

Investigated quasi-normal modes (QNMs) of Ellis–Bronnikov wormholes under perturbative analysis to study their characteristic oscillation and damping behaviour.
